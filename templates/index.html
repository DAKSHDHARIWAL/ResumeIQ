What gets analyzed

ATS & structure check

Detects whether Summary, Education, Experience, Skills, and Projects sections are present and labeled clearly.

Skill extraction

Matches your resume against a 150+ term lexicon spanning languages, frameworks, cloud, data/ML, and soft skills.

Writing quality

Flags weak phrasing like "responsible for", checks how many bullets start with strong action verbs, and rewards quantified impact.

Job description match

Paste any JD to get a keyword overlap score plus a TF-IDF similarity score, and a list of exactly what's missing.
